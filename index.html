Standard Pickup Rules

  • 01 Games are 5v5 full court (or 3v3 half court by agreement)
  • 02 Win by 2 or first to 21 (your call at tip-off)
  • 03 Check ball at the top of the key. No cherry picking.
  • 04 3-second violation strictly enforced on offense
  • 05 Call your own fouls. Respect the game.

Automated Matching Protocol

  • 01 Teams are auto-balanced by skill level (avg difference < 0.8)
  • 02 Position diversity prioritized (no more than 2 of same position per team when possible)
  • 03 Proximity + availability taken into account for court suggestion
  • 04 Skill range capped: highest and lowest on same team differ by max 3 points
  • 05 Re-roll anytime. AI learns from accepted matches over time.
